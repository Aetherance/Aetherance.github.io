Hello, I’m Aether. I’m interested in distributed systems, storage engines, and backend infrastructure. This blog documents the design, implementation, and ideas behind that work.

My pinned GitHub projects include work around Apache Dubbo Go, the distributed key-value store taluskv, and the embeddable LSM-tree storage engine talusdb.
